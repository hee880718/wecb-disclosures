# Where Export Controls Bind — public source documents (supplement 2)

(Zenodo DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23636445; github.com/hee880718/wecb-replication). Every file was downloaded from a public portal —
SEC EDGAR, EDINET, OpenDART, cninfo, the Shanghai Stock Exchange, company investor-relations sites, GACC (China customs),
Korea Customs Service, Taiwan ITA and the US Consolidated Screening List — and is reproduced here for research provenance.

## Groups

- **01–04 Chinese filings (cninfo, company sites)** — annual and quarterly reports of the Chinese toolmakers (NAURA, AMEC, Piotech, Kingsemi, Changchuan, Jingce), foundries (SMIC, Hua Hong), packagers (JCET, TFMC, Huatian), ZTE and Huawei; Entity-List firms and never-designated controls for the designation tests.
- **05–08 SEC EDGAR** — 10-K/10-Q/20-F filings and XBRL companyfacts for the US-listed equipment firms (donors and compliance tests) and for the telecom/device peers used as ZTE and Huawei donors.
- **09–15 Japan, Korea and Europe** — EDINET securities reports, OpenDART annual reports, AIXTRON/ASM International investor relations, ASML annual reports and quarterly packages, Tokyo Electron earnings pages, Hua Hong quarterly results.
- **16–18 Memory makers** — YMTC and CXMT STAR Market prospectuses, sponsor letters and audit reports (Appendix E of the paper).
- **19 GACC** — China customs monthly imports and exports by trading partner at the eight-digit line (848620xx tool lines, 8542, 847130, 847131, 851714, 851762).
- **20 Sanctions registers** — US Consolidated Screening List extract (Entity List entries, Federal Register notices, dates).
- **21–22 Other customs statistics** — Korea Customs Service HSK trade tables (parquet) and Taiwan ITA exports of HS 8542.
- **23 Tracker press releases** — archived press releases of Canalys, Omdia, IDC and Counterpoint used for handset-unit figures (short text files), with the panel built from them.

## How to use

Unzip the assets you need; file names inside keep the issuer's naming. The processed series derived from these documents, the extraction rules and the audit log are in the replication package (`registry/` and Appendix F of the paper).
